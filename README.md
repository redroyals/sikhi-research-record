## Attribution

Sukhjeet's recorded work includes AI OCR methodologies, library-wide AI-assisted translation and audit workflows, source alignment, corpus expansion and platform engineering. His workflows may use his own translation approach or build on credited translators' approaches.

Jasvant is recorded as SikhiUni's founder and SikhLibrary's Hugging Face founder and maintainer, with book and data contributions. Both contribute data to SikhLibrary. Follow its [published joint citation](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jsdosanj/SikhLibrary#how-to-cite) when using it.

Original authors, books, source editions, editors and existing translators retain their attribution and rights. This record does not replace their citations or establish ownership of their work.
